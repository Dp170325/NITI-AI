## Covered Schemes & Portals

### Central Government Flagships
1. **Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)**
   - Ministry: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)
   - Implementing Agency: KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)
   - Maximum Subsidy: Up to 35% in rural areas, 25% in urban areas.
   - Eligible Sectors: Manufacturing (up to ₹50 Lakhs), Services (up to ₹20 Lakhs).

2. **Stand-Up India Scheme for Women and SC/ST Entrepreneurs**
   - Ministry: Ministry of Finance
   - Implementing Agency: SIDBI / Scheduled Commercial Banks
   - Loan Quantum: ₹10 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore for greenfield enterprises.

3. **Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)**
   - Shishu: Up to ₹50,000
   - Kishore: ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000
   - Tarun: ₹5,00,000 to ₹10,00,000 (Tarun Plus up to ₹20 Lakhs)
   - Collateral: Zero collateral required.

4. **Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)**
   - Guarantee coverage up to 85% for micro & small enterprises without collateral.
